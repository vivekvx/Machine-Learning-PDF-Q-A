The exact [commit archive](https://codeload.github.com/python/cpython/tar.gz/e848e4b09ca4bc67c0041c9ce7526dae471d4c36) was fetched on **2026-10-12 UTC** and retained at `source/cpython-e848e4b.tar.gz`, SHA-256 `cc32fd4a62ecbc76d60df1d238531dada68d6b57423037222c250ca0f7e41cf2`. **Required attribution and notices for any redistribution:** retain the full PSF License Version 2 and its enumerated-year PSF copyright notice, retain the pinned documentation notice “Copyright © 2001-2023 Python Software Foundation; All Rights Reserved,” attribute Python Software Foundation and the named page authors recorded in source headers, and describe the corpus transformations. The transformations were: select conservative unindented prose paragraphs from `Doc/library`, `Doc/tutorial`, and `Doc/reference`; reject examples, directives, code, figures, tables, URLs, quotations, medical terms, and explicit rights-notice paragraphs; normalize visible inline RST; group related/near-duplicate pages; remove six duplicate or near-duplicate paragraphs; partition by group; fit a byte BPE tokenizer on training text only. Each retained paragraph records source line numbers and a cleaned-text hash in its split JSONL row.

## Inclusion decisions and limits

- **236 official documentation pages** passed source-level and prose selection as candidates. **234** remained after deduplication: 193 train, 21 validation, and 20 sealed test pages. All have pinned source hashes and URLs. The final test file is sealed for training and model selection.
- **112 pages** were excluded before partitioning: 105 had fewer than two eligible prose paragraphs, six matched a conservative risk list for external specifications or third-party components (`decimal`, `sqlite3`, `ssl`, `tkinter`, `turtle`, `ctypes`), and one (`abc.rst`) had an explicit adaptation statement near its header. Two more pages had fewer than two paragraphs after duplicate removal. These records remain in the ledger; no source was silently deleted.
- Paragraph-level structural filtering rejected code/indented blocks, page furniture, external links/quotations, unclear inline RST, and detached references to removed tables or examples. No selected title or retained prose paragraph matched the configured medical-keyword exclusion rule. This is an automated screen, not a full human subject-matter review.
- No third-party repository, figure, recipe, code example, quoted specification, or external linked text was added. The official PSF documentation statement is verified for the pinned source; individual contributor chain of title and whether a downstream model artifact needs additional rights review were **not independently adjudicated**. Treat those questions as unresolved before distributing model weights or a derivative corpus beyond this local research workflow.

The [v3 config](config.json) defines the exact inclusion and exclusion rules. The [grouping audit](grouping_audit.json) and [leakage audit](leakage_audit.json) record split formation and lexical overlap checks. They do not establish absence of semantic paraphrases or shared technical facts.
